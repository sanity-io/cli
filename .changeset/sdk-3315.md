`app.icon` in `defineApplication` can now point at any PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, SVG, BMP, TIFF, HEIF or AVIF image of up to 5 MB. Deploying uploads it as the application's icon, and deploying without `app.icon` removes it. `sanity dev` and `sanity start` show the image in Dashboard from the local server.
